Enter how many empty cans you have. Pick anything from the list and follow the animated guide, with tools and safety notes for each build.

Zero-waste planner algorithm

Solves a 0/1 knapsack over the whole library: use as many of your cans as possible, then fit in as many different projects as possible, then prefer easier builds.

AI maker assistant powered by Claude

Describe what you have and want. The assistant picks from the library or sketches a custom build. AI suggestions can be wrong, so always follow the safety notes.
